Personal Development

Personal Development (or PerDev) is a core subject in the Senior High School (Grade 12) curriculum, a lifelong, conscious process of improving your self-awareness, skills, talents, and character traits and it also helps how you see yourself and other people as a whole instead of just judging them based on want you saw or heard.

What you will find here

Lessons

Explore the lessons in Personal Development to better understand yourself, develop positive values, improve your relationships, and build essential life skills. Each lesson will help you grow as a person and prepare for the challenges and responsibilities of everyday life.

Activities

Test your understanding of the lessons through questions and activities. Answer each question to check what you have learned, strengthen your knowledge, and identify areas where you can improve.
