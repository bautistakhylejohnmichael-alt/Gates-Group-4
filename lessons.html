    Week 1: Introduction to Personal Development

    Personal development is a lifelong, conscious process of improving your self-awareness, skills, talents, and character traits. It helps you understand yourself better, develop positive values, improve your relationships, and build essential life skills. By focusing on personal development, you can grow as a person and prepare for the challenges and responsibilities of everyday life.

    View Lesson

    Week 2: Knowing Oneself

    Knowing oneself is a crucial aspect of personal development. It involves understanding your own thoughts, emotions, values, and behaviors. By gaining self-awareness, you can make informed decisions, set meaningful goals, and build stronger relationships with others.

    View Lesson

    Week 3: Developing The Whole Person

    Developing the whole person involves nurturing various aspects of your being, including physical, emotional, intellectual, social, and spiritual dimensions. By focusing on holistic development, you can achieve a balanced and fulfilling life.

    View Lesson

    Week 4: Developmental Stages in Middle and Late Adolescence

    Adolescence is an important stage where teenagers discover their identity, develop their beliefs, improve their thinking skills, and learn to become independent. According to Erik Erikson, the main challenge during this stage is Identity vs. Role Confusion. Through experimentation and social relationships, teenagers gradually understand who they are and what they want to become.
