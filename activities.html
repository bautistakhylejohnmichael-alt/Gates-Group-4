Activities

Activities are designed to help you apply what you've learned in the lessons and deepen your understanding of personal development. Each activity will challenge you to reflect on your experiences, identify areas for growth, and develop strategies for personal improvement.

Activity 1

This is the first activity for the Personal Development course.

Instructions: Answer the following questions based on your own experiences.

1. What are three words that best describe you?
2. What are your strengths and weaknesses?
3. What are your goals for the future?
4. What values or beliefs are important to you?
5. What kind of person do you want to become?

Activity 2

This is the second activity for the Personal Development course.

Instructions: Reflect on your personal development journey and answer the following questions.

1. What have you learned about yourself in the past year?
2. How have you grown as a person?
3. What challenges have you faced and how did you overcome them?
4. What are your plans for continued personal growth?

Activity 3

This is the third activity for the Personal Development course.

Instructions: Identify which aspect of holistic development is being described. Choose from the following:

- Physiological
- Cognitive
- Psychological
- Social
- Spiritual

1. Getting enough sleep and eating healthy foods.
2. Thinking carefully before making decisions.
3. Managing emotions when facing problems.
4. Building good relationships with friends and family.
5. Practicing honesty and following personal values

Activity 4

This is the fourth activity for the Personal Development course.

Instructions: Match the following developmental stages with their corresponding challenges. Write the letter of the correct challenge next to each stage.

1. Infancy (0-2 years)
2. Early Childhood (3-5 years)
3. Middle Childhood (6-11 years)
4. Adolescence (12-18 years)
5. Young Adulthood (19-40 years)

A. Developing a sense of identity and independence
B. Learning to trust caregivers and form attachments
C. Developing social skills and forming friendships
D. Learning to control emotions and develop self-discipline
E. Establishing intimate relationships and pursuing career goals
